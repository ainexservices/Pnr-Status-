FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about PNR status checking.

What is a PNR number? +

PNR is a 10-digit number associated with your railway reservation.

Where can I find my PNR number? +

Your PNR number can be found on your railway ticket or booking confirmation.

What information can I see? +

Depending on the available railway data, the result can include train, journey and passenger booking/current status information.

Can I check PNR from mobile? +

Yes. AINEX E Ticket is designed with a mobile-first responsive interface.
