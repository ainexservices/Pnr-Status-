FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PNR number?

PNR is a 10-digit number associated with your railway reservation. It can be used to check ticket status.

How can I check my PNR?

Enter your 10-digit PNR number in the search box and tap Check Status.

Will passenger status be displayed?

Passenger booking and current status information returned by the railway data service will be displayed.

Can I check another PNR?

Yes. After viewing a result, use the Check Another PNR button to perform another search.
