Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PNR number?

PNR is a 10-digit number associated with your railway reservation.

Where can I find my PNR?

You can find your PNR number on your railway ticket or booking confirmation.

What information will be shown?

Train details, journey route, journey date and passenger booking/current status.
